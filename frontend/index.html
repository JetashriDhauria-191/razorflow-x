As autonomous AI buyers consume the internet, merchants face 3 vulnerabilities: Unstructured Catalogs lacking vector intent scoring, Ungated AI Spending risking runaway charges, and Fragile Payment Pipes failing on network drops. RAZORFLOW X provides an end-to-end agentic protocol with 114+ multi-category SKU search, 8-factor money safety guardrails, atomic idempotency, and self-healing recovery.

DISCOVER (5-Factor Intent Scoring) → RECOMMEND (Multi-Attribute AI Score) → CONVERT (Cross-Sell Combo) → PROTECT (8-Factor Safety Gate & Idempotency) → RECOVER (FSM Self-Healing) → GROW (Merchant Telemetry).
